Privacy Policy

Version 2.4 | Last Updated Feb 2026

Our Commitment

At Amber & Co., we understand that the privacy of your data is as valuable as the diamonds we value. This policy outlines how we protect your private information and valuation data.

Data Collection

We collect only the metrics necessary for neural-grade diamond estimation, including carat weight, cut, color, and clarity coordinates. We do not store personal identification unless you create a Private Office account.

Encryption Standard

All valuation sessions are encrypted using AES-256 standards, the same used by the world's leading financial and diamond exchanges.

Third-Party Disclosure

Your data is never sold. We share anonymized diamond metrics with global exchanges (Antwerp/Tel Aviv) solely to ensure market-rate accuracy for our clients.
